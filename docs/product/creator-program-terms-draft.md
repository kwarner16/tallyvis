# TallyVis Founding Creator Program — draft participant terms

**STATUS: DRAFT. Not published anywhere, not shown to any creator, and
not legally reviewed.** This exists so Kyle has a concrete starting point
to send to a lawyer (or decide to simplify himself) before actually
handing program terms to a real creator. Nothing in this document should

## 1. What this program is

TallyVis's Founding Creator Program is an invitation/application-based
affiliate arrangement between TallyVis and an individual creator
("Creator"). A Creator who promotes TallyVis using their unique referral
link may earn a commission on subscription revenue from businesses that
sign up through that link, and (if the Creator also operates their own
qualifying business) may receive complimentary access to TallyVis while
actively partnered.

## 2. Commission

- **Rate and duration**: TallyVis's standard offer is a 20% recurring
  commission on eligible collected subscription revenue, for the first
  12 months of each referred, paying customer's subscription. TallyVis
  may set a different rate/duration for a specific Creator by agreement.
- **"Eligible collected revenue"** means the amount TallyVis's payment
  processor (Stripe) actually collects for a referred business's
  recurring subscription invoice, after any discount/coupon, and
  excludes TallyVis's own one-time fees (such as a one-time installation
  fee) and any payment that is later refunded.
- Commission is calculated and recorded automatically when TallyVis's
  payment processor confirms an invoice was paid, and reversed
  automatically if that same payment is later refunded.
- **\[Needs Kyle/legal input\]**: what happens to a commission already
  marked "paid" by Kyle if a refund arrives afterward — this draft

## 3. Payment of commission

- TallyVis does not currently pay commissions automatically. Kyle
  reviews and manually pays creators on a basis communicated separately
  (e.g. monthly), and separately marks each paid commission as such in
  TallyVis's internal system.
- **\[Needs Kyle/legal input\]**: minimum payout threshold (if any),
  payment method(s) offered, and whether a tax form (e.g. a US Form
  W-9) is collected before any payment — none of this is built or
  decided yet.

## 4. Complimentary TallyVis access

- If offered, a Creator's own qualifying business may use TallyVis at no
  charge while the Creator remains an active program participant.
- This access is tied to the Creator's active status and may be
  suspended or ended at TallyVis's discretion, including immediately
  upon pausing/ending the Creator's program participation.
- **\[Needs Kyle/legal input\]**: whether any minimum notice is promised
  before revoking complimentary access — this draft does not promise
  any.

## 5. Disclosure obligations

The Creator is solely responsible for clearly and conspicuously
disclosing their material connection to TallyVis (e.g. "#ad," "TallyVis
partner," or equivalent plain-language disclosure) wherever and whenever
they promote TallyVis or their referral link, in accordance with
applicable advertising/endorsement law and platform rules in their own
jurisdiction and on each platform they use. TallyVis does not provide
legal advice to Creators about how to comply.

## 6. No income guarantee

TallyVis makes no promise or guarantee about how much, if anything, a
Creator will earn through this program. Past results of any other
creator are not a guarantee of future results.

## 7. Term and termination

TallyVis may modify, pause, or end this program, or a specific
Creator's participation in it, at any time and for any reason, with
referrals already attributed before that point keeping whatever
commission they've already earned under these terms unless TallyVis
determines the referral was fraudulent or in violation of these terms.
